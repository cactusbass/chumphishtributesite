Welcome to Chum News

We're excited to launch our new news section! This is where we'll share updates from the band, show recaps, behind-the-scenes moments, and anything else we think you might enjoy.

Chum has been bringing Phish to the Bay Area for years now, and we're grateful for every single person who's come out to catch a show, listened to a recording on Archive.org, or spread the word to a friend.

What to Expect

Here's what you'll find in our news section:

  • Show announcements — Be the first to know when we're playing
  • Recaps — Stories and setlists from recent shows
  • New recordings — When we drop new audio on Archive.org
  • Behind the scenes — Band updates, gear talk, and more

Stay Connected

Don't want to miss anything? Here's how to stay in the loop:

  • Sign up for our mailing list
  • Follow us on Instagram
  • Like us on Facebook
  • Check our recordings on Archive.org

Thanks for being part of the Chum community. See you at a show!

— The Band
