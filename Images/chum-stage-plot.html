Signal Flow

All inputs go to our Aviom AN-16/i-M via XLR.

FOH: Individual XLR outputs from our Aviom to your board.

Monitors: We use Aviom personal mixers with IEMs. No wedges needed.

FOH Provides

  • 16 XLR runs from stage to board (+ ~5 more if available)
  • Drum mics (kick, snare, hi-hat, toms, overheads)

Power Requirements

We need power drops (multiple outlets) at:

  • Stage left — Keys
  • Center — Guitar
  • Stage right — Bass
  • Back of stage — Drums

Notes for FOH

  • No wedges required — we run IEMs from our Aviom system
  • Keys are silent on stage — no amp, just stereo DI
  • Guitar has 2 amps (wet/dry) — both mic'd, we bring them
  • Bass DI — Avalon U5 (we bring it)
  • Drums are fully mic'd for our IEM mix — FOH can use as many or as few as needed
