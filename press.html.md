# CHUM — Official Press Kit

> Chum is one of the most tenured Phish tribute acts in the country, bringing improvisational magic to the San Francisco Bay Area since 2010.

## About
Chum has wowed audiences with incredible versions of Phish's most intricate compositions while routinely creating improvisational magic in the spirit of the band they love. Known for their expansive library of Phish covers across eras 1.0 to 4.0, they will tackle any challenge you can think up.

> "Very few bands out there even dare to embrace the intricate discipline of executing these songs properly. San Francisco's Chum has earned elite status amongst the Phish community with their rare ability to capture the essence of the band's razor-sharp precision, organic improvisation and playful spirit. From 1.0 to 4.0, they have it all dialed. Even the most jaded vets nod in approval."
> — Tyler Blue, veteran music promoter

## Repertoire & Notable Past Venues
Across 200+ shows since 2010, Chum has built a 200+ song live repertoire spanning 140+ Phish originals (including *You Enjoy Myself*, *The Divided Sky*, *Reba*, *Fluffhead*, *Guyute*, *The Curtain With*, *It's Ice*, *Foam*, *Esther*, and *Colonel Forbin's Ascent > Fly Famous Mockingbird*) alongside 70+ classic rock, funk, progressive rock, new wave, Grateful Dead, and bluegrass covers across 35+ artists: Talking Heads, Grateful Dead, The Rolling Stones, The Beatles / John Lennon, Led Zeppelin, David Bowie, Pink Floyd, The Who, Frank Zappa, Little Feat, Boston, The Allman Brothers Band, Stevie Wonder, ZZ Top, The Cars, The Velvet Underground, Jimi Hendrix, Paul Simon, Warren Zevon, Ween, TV on the Radio, Bob Marley, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard.

- **Flagship Concert Halls & Clubs:** Great American Music Hall (SF), The UC Theatre (Berkeley), The Chapel (SF), The Catalyst (Santa Cruz), Terrapin Crossroads Grate Room (San Rafael), Crystal Bay Casino (Tahoe), Cornerstone (Berkeley), Felton Music Hall, Moe's Alley (Santa Cruz), HopMonk Tavern (Novato & Sebastopol), Boom Boom Room (SF), Brick & Mortar Music Hall (SF), 19 Broadway & Peri's (Fairfax), Ivy Room (Albany), Crazy Horse (Nevada City), Saint Rocke (Los Angeles).
- **Festivals, Corporate & Private Events:** Dreamforce (Salesforce Conference), Oakland Ballers Stadium, Las Vegas Phish Sphere After-Parties, Commodore SF Bay Boat Cruise, Breweries & Wineries (Woodhouse, HopMonk), Weddings (Moraga & Marin), Milestone 50th Birthdays & Block Parties.
